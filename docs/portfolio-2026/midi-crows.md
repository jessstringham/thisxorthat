In the evenings, thousands of crows gather in downtown Portland to roost. It's a delight to watch: crows, a particularly intelligent and social bird, seem like they're having fun soaring in groups between the buildings.

I've taken several approaches to visualizing the patterns made by the flock.

<a href="https://youtube.com/shorts/YgEarcjAwX0"><img class="floatmedimage" src="/assets/imgs/portfolio-2026/crow-midi.jpeg" alt="Composite image of crows location on a rooftop over time."></a>

As one output, I created an image that repeats their positions across a rooftop. It visualizes how the crows arrive, leave, and move around, as the sunset changes behind them.


Then, I used crow movement in a hand-annotated video to trigger MIDI notes. I designed the sounds in PureData. [Watch the video here](https://youtube.com/shorts/YgEarcjAwX0).
