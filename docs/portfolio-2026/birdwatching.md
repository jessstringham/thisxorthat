A thesis exploring art-making and parameter space exploration using the metaphors of birdwatching.


> What can we learn from birdwatching as an observational practice to inform computational art? Can making computational art cultivate our habits of observation? First, I analyze birdwatching and put forth principles and a feedback loop of observing and creating. Next, I apply the principles to my own practice, creating seven computational sketches from nature, which cover several algorithmic techniques and natural phenomena, and evaluate the experience. Finally, I translate these principles into tools for exploring computational art systems. I frame exploring a system as a nature walk: rather than a model to be controlled or optimized, it becomes an unpredictable landscape to navigate, one that invites discovery and noticing. Together, these investigations uncover new ways of seeing and making with code.

The full thesis is available [here](https://media.thisxorthat.dev/stringham-jessicas-SM-MAS-2026-thesis.pdf).
